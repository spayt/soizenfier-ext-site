# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small business owners across Canada — English and French-speaking — who need a professional web presence but are non-technical. They're evaluating agencies cautiously: they've seen bad experiences (overpriced, hard to reach, hidden fees). They land on this site while comparison-shopping. Primary job: decide whether to trust SoiZenFier enough to send a message or book a call.

## Product Purpose

SoiZenFier Technologies is a Canadian web agency offering a complete, managed web stack — website design and development, hosting, monthly maintenance, and SEO monitoring — as a single ongoing relationship. The site's job is to turn a skeptical small business owner into a confident lead. Success = form submission or plan selection.

## Brand Personality

Professional · Trustworthy · Modern. The tone is a competent contractor who shows up on time and explains things clearly — not a tech startup trying to impress other developers. Warmth comes from clarity and reliability, not from friendliness performance.

## Differentiator

Full-stack, one partner: clients get a website built, hosted, maintained, and monitored for SEO without juggling vendors. Bilingual (EN/FR) service is a secondary but real advantage in the Canadian market. Transparent fixed pricing — no surprise invoices — is a trust signal that closes deals with SMB owners burned before.

## Anti-references

- Generic Wix / Squarespace template aesthetic — cookie-cutter grids, stock photo heroes, thin typography
- Overly dark hacker / cyberpunk — intimidates the target client; signals "for developers, not for you"
- Corporate consulting (McKinsey-style) — cold, jargon-heavy, suit-and-tie energy; inaccessible for SMBs
- Saturated SaaS clones (Stripe / Linear aesthetic) — product-first design language; wrong register for a service company

## Design Principles

1. **Earn trust through craft** — The visual quality of this site is the portfolio. Every component demonstrates what SoiZenFier can build for a client. Sloppy spacing or generic components undermine the core value proposition.
2. **One clear path per page** — Every surface leads toward a single action: contact, get a quote, or select a plan. Design reduces friction on that path; it doesn't add scenic detours.
3. **Approachable professionalism** — Modern and sharp, but never cold. A small business owner should feel capable of working with this team — not outclassed by it.
4. **French–English parity** — Both language versions get equal polish. Bilingual is a differentiator, not a footnote; the FR experience must feel as intentional as the EN one.
5. **Show, don't template** — Avoid AI-default scaffolding (eyebrow labels on every section, identical card grids, hero-metric blocks). Each section earns its structure from what it communicates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best-effort approach. Maintain readable contrast on all body text (≥4.5:1), ensure keyboard navigability for forms and dialogs, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for any animations added in future. No strict WCAG level committed, but don't ship obviously broken accessibility.
